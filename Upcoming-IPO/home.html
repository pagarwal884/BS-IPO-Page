Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions that come in your mind related to IPO investing and subscription process.

How to Subscribe to an IPO?

  • Step 1: Login to your respective service provider.
  • Step 2: Click on the IPO button
  • Step 3: Select the IPO and enter the details
  • Step 4: Make payment or authorize mandate
Tip: Ensure you have sufficient funds in your account before applying.

Should I buy an IPO on the first day?

  • If oversubscribed, IPOs often list at a premium.
  • IPOs from known, profitable brands perform well.
  • Always read the DRHP and understand the risks.
Pros
  • Potential for high listing gains
  • Early entry into promising companies
Cons
  • Potential for listing below issue price
  • Market volatility can affect performance

What documents are required for IPO application?

  • PAN card (mandatory for all applicants)
  • Demat account details
  • Bank account linked with your trading account
  • KYC documents (if not already completed)
Note: Some brokers may require additional documents for first-time applicants.
